About

Curiosity came first. Everything else followed.

It started small — wondering how the apps on my phone actually worked underneath, and what happened between typing something and seeing it appear on screen. That question followed me through my last years of high school in Bandar Lampung, where I spent more time than I'd admit tinkering with logic puzzles and half-finished scripts instead of just using the software other people built.

When I enrolled in Informatics Engineering at Institut Teknologi Sumatera, that curiosity finally had a proper structure to grow in. I began the way most people do — with the basics of Python, then C++, then the slow, sometimes frustrating, often satisfying process of actually understanding data structures instead of memorizing them. Somewhere in that process, a more specific interest started to form: I kept gravitating toward the questions that involved data — how it's stored, moved, cleaned, and made useful at scale.

Right now, my focus is on strengthening that foundation: databases, Python, and the fundamentals of how systems handle information responsibly and efficiently. The dream ahead is to become a Data Engineer — someone who builds the quiet infrastructure that lets other people's ideas run on good data. I'm not there yet, and I don't pretend to be. But I like knowing exactly where I'm headed, and I like the work of getting there.
